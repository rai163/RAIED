Impedance matching (ceramic to projectile): Z = ρ × V (wave speed) For Al₂O₃: Z = 3.90 × 10³ × 10,500 = 41 × 10⁶ kg/m²·s For steel projectile: Z = 7.85 × 10³ × 5,100 = 40 × 10⁶ kg/m²·s → Near-perfect impedance match maximizes stress transfer Dwell time (ceramic failure): t_dwell = L / V_c For 6mm ceramic, V_c ≈ 8,000 m/s (crack speed) → t_dwell = 0.75 μs During dwell, projectile erodes, reducing length by 20-40% Kinetic energy absorption distribution: - Steel strike face: 15-20% - Ceramic comminution: 25-30% - Backing laminate: 40-45% - Adhesive interlayer: 5-10%
3. MANUFACTURING PROCESS
Step 1: Ceramic Tile Production (8 hours per batch)
  • Powder preparation: Al₂O₃ (99.5%, 0.5μm particle size) + 0.5% MgO sintering aid
  • Spray drying: Form spherical agglomerates (100-200μm diameter)
  • Cold isostatic pressing (CIP): 200 MPa, produces green body 55×55×6.5mm (allows for shrinkage)
  • Sintering: 1600°C for 4 hours (10°C/min ramp, 2°C/min cooling from 1000°C)
  • Final dimensions: 50×50×6mm ±0.05mm, hardness >1400 HV
Step 2: Backing Laminate Preparation (6 hours)
  • Pre-preg: 12 layers aramid fabric (400 g/m²) impregnated with epoxy (40% resin by weight)
  • Lay-up sequence: Cross-ply orientation (0°/90°/0°/90°) for balanced properties
  • Consolidation: Vacuum bag at -0.95 bar, heat to 80°C for 1 hour, cool to room temperature
  • Trim to 2.7m × 1.5m (shutter dimensions) by water-jet
Step 3: Adhesive Application & Tile Placement (3 hours)
  • Mix epoxy adhesive (LOCTITE EA 9394) at 100:17 resin:hardener ratio
  • Apply 0.5-1.0mm adhesive to backing laminate using notched trowel (3mm notches)
  • Place ceramic tiles in offset pattern (like brick laying), 0.5mm gaps between tiles
  • Apply 0.5mm adhesive over tiles (flood coat)
  • Position steel face plate (2mm RHA), apply 50kg/m² weight to ensure contact
Step 4: Vacuum Bagging & Curing (8 hours)
  • Place assembly in vacuum bag, apply -0.95 bar vacuum
  • Transfer to autoclave: Ramp 25→120°C at 2°C/min, hold 120°C for 3 hours, apply 5 bar pressure
  • Cool to 60°C at 1°C/min, then release pressure, cool to room temperature
Step 5: Edge Sealing & Anti-Spall Coating
  • Route edges to remove excess adhesive, apply polyurethane sealant (1-2mm)
  • Spray polyurea on back face (2mm thickness), extends 25mm onto edges
  • Cure: 23°C for 24 hours
