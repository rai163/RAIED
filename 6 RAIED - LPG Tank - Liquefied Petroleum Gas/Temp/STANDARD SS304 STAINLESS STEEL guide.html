3. STEP-BY-STEP MANUFACTURING PROCESS
Step 1: Melting & Casting (8 hours)

Equipment: Electric Arc Furnace (EAF, 100 ton capacity) + Argon Oxygen Decarburization (AOD) vessel

  • Charge: 70% scrap stainless steel + 30% ferrochrome/nickel alloys
  • EAF melt: 1600°C, 4 hours, slag basicity ratio (CaO/SiO₂) = 1.8-2.2 for P removal
  • AOD refining: Decarburization to <0.08% C using O₂/Ar mixture (1:3 ratio), 2 hours, 1650°C
  • Deoxidation: Add Si (0.5 kg/ton) and Al (0.3 kg/ton)
  • Continuous casting: Slab size 200mm thick × 2m wide, casting speed 1.2 m/min
Step 2: Hot Rolling (2 hours)
  • Reheat slab to 1250°C (soak 30 minutes, furnace atmosphere reducing to minimize scale)
  • Roughing mill (4 passes): 200 → 100mm, temperature drop to 1150°C
  • Finishing mill (7 stands): 100 → 8mm, exit temperature 900°C
  • Laminar cooling: Water spray cooling at 30°C/sec to 600°C
  • Coiling: Coil weight 20 tons, cooling to room temperature in 48 hours
Step 3: Cold Rolling & Annealing (4 hours)
  • Pickling: HNO₃/HF bath (15% HNO₃ + 3% HF, 55°C, 10 minutes) to remove scale
  • Cold rolling (5 passes): 8mm → 6mm → 4.5mm → 3.5mm → 2.5mm → final 2mm
  • Solution annealing: 1050°C for 5 minutes (bright annealing in H₂ atmosphere)
  • Water quenching: 30°C, cooling rate >100°C/sec to prevent sensitization
Step 4: Cutting & Finishing
