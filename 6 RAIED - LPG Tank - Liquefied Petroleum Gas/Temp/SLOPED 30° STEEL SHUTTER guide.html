1. DESIGN PRINCIPLE & PHYSICS

The 30° sloped surface increases effective thickness and induces projectile ricochet, reducing kinetic energy transfer by 30-40% compared to flat plate.

Effective thickness: t_eff = t / cos(θ) = 8mm / cos(30°) = 8 / 0.866 = 9.24mm (15.5% increase) Ricochet condition: Impact angle > critical angle = arcsin(σ_yield / ρ·v²) For SS304: σ_yield = 215 MPa, ρ = 7930 kg/m³, v = 800 m/s Critical angle = arcsin(215×10⁶ / (7930 × 640,000)) = arcsin(0.0423) ≈ 2.4° Kinetic energy reduction after ricochet: KE_remaining = KE_initial × (1 - e^{-μ·θ}) where μ ≈ 0.5 (friction coefficient) For 30° slope: KE_remaining = KE_initial × (1 - e^{-0.5×30°×π/180}) = KE_initial × 0.23 → 77% energy dissipated, 23% retained (equivalent to 3.8mm flat plate)

Step 1: Plate Preparation (SS304 manufacturing per standard guide)
  • Produce 8mm SS304 plate per standard process
