3. MANUFACTURING PROCESS
Step 1: Batching & Mixing (45 minutes per batch)
  • Weigh all materials to ±0.5% accuracy (loss-in-weight feeders)
  • Mix dry materials (cement, silica fume, sand) in pan mixer at 30 RPM for 2 minutes
  • Add 80% of water + superplasticizer, mix at 50 RPM for 3 minutes
  • Add steel fibers (slowly, over 2 minutes) to prevent balling
  • Add remaining water to adjust slump, mix additional 3 minutes at 70 RPM
  • Target: Self-compacting slurry, slump flow 700-800mm (ASTM C1611)
Step 2: Casting (15 minutes per panel)
  • Mold preparation: Steel mold (6m × 3m × 8mm), surface ground to Ra <0.5μm, apply release agent
  • Vibration: External vibrators (60 Hz, 0.5mm amplitude) applied for 2 minutes
  • Surface finishing: Vacuum dewatering to remove excess water from surface
  • Covering: Polyethylene sheet immediately after casting to prevent evaporation
Step 3: Curing (48 hours heat cure or 28 days ambient)

Option A: Heat Cure (accelerated)

  • 24 hours at 23°C, 95% RH (initial set)
  • Steam cure: Ramp to 90°C at 15°C/hour, hold 48 hours, cool at 15°C/hour
  • Autoclave (optional): 180°C, 8 bar for 4 hours → compressive strength 220 MPa+

Option B: Ambient Cure (standard)

  • 28 days at 23°C ±2°C, >95% RH (under wet burlap or curing blankets)
  • Spray curing compound after 7 days to retain moisture
Step 4: Finishing (2 hours)
  • Grinding: Diamond cup wheel (120 grit), remove 0.5mm surface, achieves ±1mm thickness tolerance
  • Drilling: CNC diamond drill (25mm diameter, 2,500 RPM, 0.5mm/sec feed) for M24 bolts
  • Edge grinding: 45° chamfer, 2mm depth
