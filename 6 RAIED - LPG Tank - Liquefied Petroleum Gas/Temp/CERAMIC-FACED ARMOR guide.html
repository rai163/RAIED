2. PHYSICS OF CERAMIC ARMOR
Projectile/target interaction (conoid formation): When ceramic is impacted, a conical fracture zone (conoid) forms beneath the impact point Conoid angle: 60-70° for Al₂O₃, 50-60° for SiC (more efficient) Fracture mechanics: K_IC (ceramic) = 4-5 MPa·m¹⁄₂ (Al₂O₃) Crack velocity: V_c ≈ 0.5 × V_s (shear wave speed) ≈ 3,000-4,000 m/s Ballistic mass efficiency (E_m): E_m = (ρ_ceramic / ρ_steel) × (t_steel / t_ceramic) for equivalent protection For Al₂O₃: ρ = 3.90, steel ρ = 7.85 → mass efficiency ≈ 3.5-4.5 Meaning: 1 kg ceramic replaces 3.5-4.5 kg steel
3. MANUFACTURING PROCESS
Step 1: Ceramic Tile Production (same as composite armor guide)
  • Al₂O₃: 50×50×6mm tiles, 1400 HV min, density 3.90 g/cm³
  • SiC: 50×50×6mm tiles, 2400 HV min, density 3.15 g/cm³ (higher performance, +50% cost)
  • Quality: Flatness ±0.05mm, edge squareness ±0.1mm
Step 2: Backing Plate Preparation
  • Cut 6061-T6 aluminum plate to 2.7m × 1.5m × 5mm (water-jet or saw)
  • Surface preparation:
    • Grit blast: 80 mesh Al₂O₃, 6 bar pressure, nozzle distance 200mm, coverage 200%
    • Result: Surface profile Ra 3-5μm
    • Solvent wipe: Acetone followed by isopropyl alcohol
    • Silane coupling agent: 1% solution (γ-glycidoxypropyltrimethoxysilane), 30 minute dwell
Step 3: Adhesive Bonding (2 hours)
  • Mix epoxy at 100:17 ratio (resin:hardener) to ±1% accuracy
  • Apply adhesive to backing plate using notched trowel (3mm notches, 60° angle)
  • Place ceramic tiles manually or with pick-and-place robot (cycle time 2 seconds per tile)
  • Tile layout: Offset pattern (brick layering), gaps 0.5mm ±0.2mm
  • Apply 50kg/m² weight over entire assembly for 24 hours at 23°C
Step 4: Vacuum Bagging & Curing
  • Place assembly in vacuum bag, apply -0.95 bar for 1 hour to remove air bubbles
  • Oven cure: 80°C for 4 hours (2°C/min ramp, 1°C/min cool)
  • Alternative: Room temperature cure for 48 hours
Step 5: Edge Sealing & Spall Liner
  • Remove excess adhesive from edges (routing or grinding)
  • Apply polyurethane edge sealant (1-2mm thickness), cure 24 hours
  • Spray polyurea on back face: 2mm thickness, extends 50mm onto edges
  • Inspect for voids or delamination (tap test or ultrasonic)
