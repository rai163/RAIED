Shutter Timing & Dual-Sheet Damage Visualization Simulator

This enhanced simulator shows shutter timing, impact energy flow, load & deflection curves, and a dual steel sheet damage visualization. Adjust parameters and press "Run Simulation" to update results.

Aim of the Simulator

The simulator aims to provide an interactive and comprehensive tool for modeling, analyzing, and visualizing the timing, energy absorption, mechanical response, and damage effects of protective shutters subjected to impact events such as drone strikes. It supports design optimization and safety assessment of multi-layer protective systems.

Key Objectives

  • Simulate Shutter Closure Timing: Model the timing of side and dome shutters closing relative to the arrival of an incoming threat, ensuring shutters close in time to provide effective protection.
  • Visualize Impact Energy Flow: Show how kinetic energy from an impact is absorbed and reduced through multiple layers, including steel sheets and air gaps, to understand energy dissipation.
  • Model Load and Deflection Curves: Represent the mechanical load and deflection response of protective layers during impact, aiding structural integrity evaluation.
  • Visualize Damage Severity: Provide a dual steel sheet damage visualization indicating areas of no damage, denting, or cracking based on impact energy and absorption.
  • Enable Interactive Parameter Adjustment: Allow users to modify key parameters such as shutter count, thickness, spacing, motor power, and detection range, and observe their effects on system performance in real-time.
  • Support Design and Safety Decisions: Assist engineers and designers in optimizing protective shutter systems for enhanced safety, reliability, and impact resistance.
