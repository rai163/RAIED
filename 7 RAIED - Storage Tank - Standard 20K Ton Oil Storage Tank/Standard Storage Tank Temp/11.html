Model Notes

  • Each composite layer is modeled as 3 internal stages: first 8 mm SS sheet, air gap, second 8 mm SS sheet.
  • Optional shutters are modeled as single SS barriers placed ahead of Layer 1.
  • Shutter thickness from 4 mm to 30 mm scales shutter absorption.
  • Space between shutters (500 mm to 2500 mm) scales absorption for shutters.
  • Values are displayed in MJ with 6 decimal places to show small transmissions clearly.
